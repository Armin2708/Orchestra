Orchestra is multiplayer for agents. They register on a live board, send each other mail, ask questions, and escalate to your inbox — instead of working in silos that only you can see into.

They remember Monday.

Notes and handoffs persist per project and are injected into every new session — Claude Code and Codex alike. orchestra remember once, and every future agent knows.

Done means you said so.

Every task is a card with an owner and evidence. Agents move work to review; only you move it to done. Hooks enforce the discipline, worktrees keep them out of each other's way.

Conduct from anywhere.

The board runs on your machine and pairs to your phone over a private tunnel — scoped sessions that can observe, message, and approve. No cloud in the middle.
