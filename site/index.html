Memory

They remember Monday.

Notes and handoffs persist per project and are injected into every new session — Claude Code and Codex alike. Say it once; every future agent on the board knows.

Review gates

Done means you said so.

Teams have roles, workflows have gates, and every card carries evidence. Agents move work to review; only a human moves it to done. Hooks enforce the discipline — worktrees keep agents out of each other's way.

Remote

Conduct from anywhere.

The board runs on your machine and pairs to your phone over a private tunnel — scoped sessions that can observe, message, and approve. No cloud in the middle, no code leaving your box.
